Selected software

Operating systems and emulation

  • With Aurora Silicon, I work on Linux support for Apple Silicon Macs. Two of my M5 patches bring up the display and USB host controllers.
  • Snapintosh boots Apple’s XNU kernel on a Snapdragon laptop. Its companion q1n1 lets me probe the hardware over USB.
  • I started exploring emulation with CHIP-8 in C. My latest emulation project is VibeStation5, an experimental PlayStation 4 and 5 runtime.
  • I work with the Omarchy M and Dragon teams to bring this Linux desktop to Macs and Snapdragon machines.

Models and tools for Apple Silicon

  • LTX-2 MLX brings Lightricks’ video generation models to the Mac. RVC MLX uses the same framework for voice conversion.
  • MacGTop lets you watch an Apple Silicon GPU from the terminal.

Markets

  • I use the Earnings Volatility Calculator to compare what the options market is pricing in before earnings with how much the stock has actually moved.
  • Testfol Margin Stresser replays historical drawdowns so I can see how leverage, margin requirements, and taxes affect a portfolio.
