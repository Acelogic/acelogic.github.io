About this site

I started mcruz.me in 2018 as a place to keep my projects and coursework.

Acknowledgment

The model for this edition is Doug Lea’s home page at SUNY Oswego. He was my professor. I like that you can follow a link from the front page straight to a paper or a piece of software. I wanted my own work to be just as easy to find.

Source

The pages are written in HTML, share a stylesheet, and are hosted on GitHub Pages. The title animations use TypeIt. The website repository includes the project catalog and links to the original course worksites.

If you find a broken link, send me an email.
