About this iPod

I'm Miguel Cruz, also known as Acelogic. This is my personal portfolio, first started in 2018 and now housed in a 3D iPod Classic.

Controls

Trace a circle around the click wheel to browse. The center button opens the highlighted item; MENU goes back. Previous and next move one item at a time.

On a keyboard, use the arrow keys, Enter, and Escape. You can also tap screen items and scroll longer project descriptions. Touch gestures on the wheel stay on the wheel.

Made in Blender

The aluminum enclosure, polished back, display, click wheel, hold switch, and connectors were modeled in Blender. The website uses the exported 3D model with a live HTML screen.

A render of the same model keeps the portfolio available when WebGL is unavailable. The static content also works without JavaScript.
